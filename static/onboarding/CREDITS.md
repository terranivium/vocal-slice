# Demo clip attribution

The first-run demo (`demo.wav`) is an excerpt from a LibriVox public-domain recording.

- **Work:** *Celtic Tales, Told to the Children* by Louey Chisholm
- **Reader:** acamerer (LibriVox volunteer)
- **Source:** https://archive.org/details/celtictalestoldtothechildren_2504_librivox
- **Rights:** Public Domain Mark 1.0 — verified on the Internet Archive item page (LibriVox recordings
  are dedicated to the public domain; the underlying text is also out of copyright).

Public Domain Mark 1.0 requires no attribution; this credit is kept as a courtesy and to record the
provenance of a redistributed asset in a paid product.

`demo.json` is the transcription of `demo.wav`, produced by running Vocal Slice's own transcription on
the clip so the word timestamps align with the audio exactly. It is not hand-authored — hand-authored
timestamps would drift from the audio and break the text→waveform mapping.
